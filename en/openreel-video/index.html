Which Mac do I have?

Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4, use Apple Silicon. If it says Processor: Intel, use Intel. Not sure? Universal works on both.

Mac says it can’t be opened?

Many open-source apps aren’t paid-signed by Apple, so the first launch shows a warning. If you downloaded it from the button above (the developer’s own release), open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Full safety checklist.

What it does

OpenReel Video is a full video editor that runs on your own device: a multi-track timeline, transitions, text animations, word-by-word karaoke subtitles, color grading, audio effects and noise removal. Your videos are never uploaded, and exports carry no watermark.

Good for

People who edit short videos for TikTok, Reels or YouTube and want CapCut-style tools without sending clips to a cloud.

Skip it if

You need pro finishing tools like Final Cut’s or a stable, finished product: it is still marked beta.

How to start

  1. Use the Mac download above, or open openreel.video in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Drag your clips onto the timeline.
  3. Add text, music and effects, then export.

Heads-up

Still in beta. The browser version needs Chrome or Edge.
