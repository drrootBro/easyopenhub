Shotcut

Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 15,769

A free, cross-platform desktop video editor for everyone. Shotcut is an open-source video editing application. It lets you import, trim, arrange, and edit your multimedia clips on a visual timeline. You can run it directly on your desktop across different operating systems.
