ShareX

Windows · ★ 41,313

Take screenshots, record screen, edit, and upload automatically. ShareX is a Windows tool for capturing screenshots and recording screen video or GIFs. It includes built-in editing, text recognition (OCR), and color picking tools. You can also set it up to save, annotate, or upload captures automatically using hotkeys.

Flameshot

Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 31,541

Take screenshots and annotate them directly on your screen. Flameshot is an easy-to-use screenshot tool for your desktop. It lets you capture specific areas or your whole screen, and annotate right away with arrows, text, shapes, or pixelation. You can then copy the image, save it locally, or upload it to Imgur.

Kap

macOS · ★ 19,427

Record your Mac screen easily from the menu bar. Kap is a clean, open-source screen recorder for Mac. It lives in your menu bar so you can capture selected areas of your screen quickly. It also supports plugins to extend functionality.
