Best CapCut Alternatives (2026)

Looking for a CapCut alternative without another subscription? These open-source apps do the same job and are ready for ordinary people to use. Tap a name for what it does, who it is for, and the official download link.

OpenCut

Web · ★ 98,479

Free and open-source video editor in your browser. OpenCut is an open-source alternative to video editors like CapCut. You can edit videos directly in your web browser without paying for a subscription. The project is actively undergoing a major rewrite to bring even more features.

OpenMotion

Web, Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 15,221

Turn reference videos into editable branded motion graphics. OpenMotion is an open-source video studio alternative to CapCut and Claude Motion. You feed it a reference video or link, and AI agents break it down into HTML-based scenes and timing. You can edit shots in a visual studio and export finished MP4 videos.
