Best Notion Alternatives (2026)

Looking for a Notion alternative without another subscription? These open-source apps do the same job and are ready for ordinary people to use. Tap a name for what it does, who it is for, and the official download link.

AppFlowy

Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS · ★ 78,876

An open-source, private workspace alternative to Notion. AppFlowy lets you manage notes, tasks, wikis, and projects in one place. It works like Notion with boards and databases, but keeps you in control of your data. You can use it across desktop and mobile devices.

Joplin

Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS · ★ 57,173

Take notes and sync them securely across your devices. Joplin is an offline-first note-taking and to-do app that stores your notes in Markdown. It supports full-text search, tags, notebooks, and importing from Evernote. You can sync notes across your devices using end-to-end encryption with Dropbox, OneDrive, Nextcloud, or Joplin Cloud.

Khoj

Web, Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS · ★ 38,125

Your personal AI second brain for search and chats. Khoj connects to your documents like PDFs, Notion, Markdown, and Word files, as well as the live web. You can ask questions, search your files, create custom AI agents, or generate images. It works as an online cloud app or can run privately on your own computer.
