Best Descript Alternatives (2026)

Looking for a Descript alternative without another subscription? These open-source apps do the same job and are ready for ordinary people to use. Tap a name for what it does, who it is for, and the official download link.

Voicebox

macOS, Windows, Linux · ★ 60,588

Local AI voice studio to clone voices and dictate text. Voicebox lets you clone voices from short audio clips and generate speech across 23 languages. It also works as a system-wide dictation tool using hotkeys to transcribe what you speak into text. Everything runs completely on your own machine without sending data to the cloud.

FunClip

Windows, macOS, Linux, Web · ★ 6,561

Clip video clips simply by selecting transcribed speech text. FunClip is an open-source tool that transcribes video audio into text using speech recognition models. It lets you cut out video highlights simply by highlighting the spoken words or picking a specific speaker. It also automatically generates SRT subtitle files for the clips.
