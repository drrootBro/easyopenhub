Excalidraw

Web · ★ 135,644

Sketch hand-drawn diagrams on a collaborative whiteboard. Excalidraw is an infinite virtual whiteboard that makes your diagrams look like hand sketches. It works right in your web browser with end-to-end encrypted collaboration. You can draw shapes, add text, and export your work as images.
