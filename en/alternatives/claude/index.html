NextChat

Jan

Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 45,713

Run private AI models completely offline on your computer. Jan is a desktop app that acts as an alternative to ChatGPT. It lets you download and run open AI models like Llama and Qwen directly on your machine without sending data to the cloud. You can also connect external services like OpenAI or Anthropic if desired.
