Best Otter.ai Alternatives (2026)

Looking for a Otter.ai alternative without another subscription? These open-source apps do the same job and are ready for ordinary people to use. Tap a name for what it does, who it is for, and the official download link.

Handy

Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 38,863

Free, offline speech-to-text dictation into any app. Handy lets you dictate text using your microphone directly into any text box on your computer. It transcribes audio locally using offline models without sending voice recordings to the cloud. You simply press a shortcut, speak, and the converted text is pasted right where you are typing.

Meetily

Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 32,686

Private, on-device AI meeting recorder and note-taker. Meetily records meeting audio, transcribes it in real time, and writes AI summaries completely on your computer. Your audio and transcripts stay local without using cloud bots or uploading recordings. You can connect it to local models via Ollama or optional cloud AI providers for summaries.

OpenWhispr

macOS, Windows, Linux · ★ 11,518

Voice-to-text dictation and meeting transcription for your desktop. OpenWhispr turns spoken words into text across any desktop app using a hotkey. It can run completely offline with local AI models so your audio never leaves your device, or connect to cloud AI. It also records meetings, recognizes speakers, and saves organized notes.

anarlog

macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android · ★ 9,959

Privacy-focused AI meeting notepad that needs no call bots. anarlog listens directly to your computer audio during meetings and writes transcripts. It can run transcription and notes locally on your device, storing everything in a local SQLite database without sending meeting bots. You can bring your own AI keys or local models for summaries.

CapsWriter-Offline

Windows · ★ 7,117

Hold CapsLock to speak and type completely offline. CapsWriter-Offline is an offline speech-to-text tool for Windows. Hold down CapsLock or a mouse button to dictate, and text appears instantly on your screen when released. It also supports converting audio or video files into subtitles and text.
