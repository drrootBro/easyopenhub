VoiceStudio

Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS · ★ 109,995

Local AI voice cloning, design, dubbing, and transcription. VoiceStudio is a desktop and mobile app that runs AI voice generation entirely on your device. You can clone voices from sample recordings, describe new voices with text, dub videos, and transcribe audio across 646 languages. It operates offline without sending your data to third-party clouds.

GPT-SoVITS

Windows, macOS, Linux, Web · ★ 64,987

Clone any voice with just one minute of audio. GPT-SoVITS lets you clone voices and generate natural speech from text using only short audio samples. A 5-second clip enables instant voice synthesis, while 1 minute of audio allows fine-tuning for much higher realism. It features a browser-based interface and supports cross-lingual generation in English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Cantonese.

Voicebox

macOS, Windows, Linux · ★ 60,588

Local AI voice studio to clone voices and dictate text. Voicebox lets you clone voices from short audio clips and generate speech across 23 languages. It also works as a system-wide dictation tool using hotkeys to transcribe what you speak into text. Everything runs completely on your own machine without sending data to the cloud.

pyVideoTrans

Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 19,753

Translate videos into other languages with AI dubbing and subtitles. pyVideoTrans converts videos from one language to another automatically. It transcribes audio into subtitles, translates the text, generates spoken voiceovers, and combines everything back into a finished video. It works with both offline models and third-party online translation or speech services.

Abogen

Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 6,228

Turn eBooks, PDFs, and text into spoken audiobooks with subtitles. Abogen is a local text-to-speech app powered by the Kokoro model. It converts documents like EPUB, PDF, and Markdown files into natural speech with matching subtitle files. It includes a desktop window and a web interface.
