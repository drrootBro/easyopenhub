Meetily

Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 32,686

Private, on-device AI meeting recorder and note-taker. Meetily records meeting audio, transcribes it in real time, and writes AI summaries completely on your computer. Your audio and transcripts stay local without using cloud bots or uploading recordings. You can connect it to local models via Ollama or optional cloud AI providers for summaries.

anarlog

macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android · ★ 9,959

Privacy-focused AI meeting notepad that needs no call bots. anarlog listens directly to your computer audio during meetings and writes transcripts. It can run transcription and notes locally on your device, storing everything in a local SQLite database without sending meeting bots. You can bring your own AI keys or local models for summaries.
