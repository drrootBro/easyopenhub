Best Audible Alternatives (2026)

Looking for a Audible alternative without another subscription? These open-source apps do the same job and are ready for ordinary people to use. Tap a name for what it does, who it is for, and the official download link.

ebook2audiobook

Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 20,550

Turn e-books into spoken audiobooks using local AI voices. ebook2audiobook transforms DRM-free ebooks and documents into audiobooks with preserved chapters. It supports over 50 text-to-speech engines, custom voice cloning, and thousands of languages. You can run it entirely on your own computer through a simple web interface.

Abogen

Windows, macOS, Linux · ★ 6,228

Turn eBooks, PDFs, and text into spoken audiobooks with subtitles. Abogen is a local text-to-speech app powered by the Kokoro model. It converts documents like EPUB, PDF, and Markdown files into natural speech with matching subtitle files. It includes a desktop window and a web interface.
