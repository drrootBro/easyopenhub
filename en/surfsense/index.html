Which Mac do I have?

Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4, use Apple Silicon. If it says Processor: Intel, use Intel. Not sure? Universal works on both.

Mac says it can’t be opened?

Many open-source apps aren’t paid-signed by Apple, so the first launch shows a warning. If you downloaded it from the button above (the developer’s own release), open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Full safety checklist.

What it does

It is a desktop app alternative to NotebookLM that runs on your computer without requiring an account. You can research, summarize, and edit PDFs, Word docs, spreadsheets, and recordings while keeping your files private. You can connect it to local models or OpenAI-compatible remote APIs.

Good for

Anyone handling confidential documents who wants a private NotebookLM alternative.

Skip it if

People happy uploading files to Google NotebookLM or cloud AI.

How to start

  1. Download and install the desktop app for your OS.
  2. Complete initial onboarding and drop your documents into sources.
  3. Ask the agent to research, extract, or edit files.

Heads-up

Office format conversions require LibreOffice, which must be installed separately.

Questions

Is SurfSense free?

Yes, SurfSense is completely free and open-source under the Apache-2.0 license. You can connect it to free local models or use your own OpenAI-compatible API keys.

Is SurfSense safe to download?

It is open source (Apache-2.0 license) with 16,868 GitHub stars, last updated 2026-11-12. Download only from the official link on this page, and scan the file before installing: see the safety checklist in our beginner guide.

Does SurfSense work offline?

Yes, SurfSense runs locally on your computer and can operate in an air-gapped, offline mode when paired with local AI models.
