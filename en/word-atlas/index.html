What it does

Word Atlas is a vocabulary learning tool containing over 7,000 IELTS words across 19 topics. Each word card includes original illustrations, UK and US audio pronunciations, and example sentences. It uses the FSRS spaced repetition system to help you review efficiently without needing an account.

Good for

People preparing for IELTS or building English vocabulary visually.

Skip it if

Anyone looking for an officially certified IELTS testing prep app.

How to start

  1. Open the official website in your browser
  2. Pick a topic to start viewing illustrated flashcards
  3. Listen to pronunciations and complete daily review quizzes
