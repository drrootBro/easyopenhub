VoiceStudio is a desktop and mobile app that runs AI voice generation entirely on your device. You can clone voices from sample recordings, describe new voices with text, dub videos, and transcribe audio across 646 languages. It operates offline without sending your data to third-party clouds.

✓ Good for

Video creators, podcasters, and anyone wanting private local voice generation.

✕ Skip it if

People who only need standard text-to-speech without custom voice cloning.

How to start

  1. Download the installer for your system from GitHub releases.
  2. Launch the app and open the Voice Cloning tab.
  3. Add a reference audio sample, enter text, and generate.

Generating speech on a CPU works but is much slower than GPU.
